# Product overview

Daylight is a simple personal life dashboard for organizing a day in one browser tab. It combines a live clock and greeting, a Pomodoro focus timer, a task list, and user-managed quick links.

## Goals
- Keep daily planning low-friction and visually calm.
- Let users manage tasks and quick links without an account or backend.
- Preserve user preferences in browser Local Storage.

## MVP requirements
- Show current local time, date, and a greeting based on time of day.
- Provide a 25-minute focus timer with start, stop/pause, and reset.
- Support adding, editing, completing, and deleting tasks.
- Persist tasks and quick links with Local Storage.
- Provide quick links that open external websites in a new tab.

## Selected enhancements
- Light/dark theme toggle.
- User-defined name in the greeting.
- Prevent duplicate task names, case-insensitively.

## Out of scope
- Backend, user accounts, cross-device synchronization, external APIs, frameworks, and build tools.
